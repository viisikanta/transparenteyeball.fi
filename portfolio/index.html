Intro

I work where games, brands and culture meet.

I spent the Angry Birds growth years at Rovio running partnerships with NASA, Barnes & Noble, 21st Century Fox and Samsung, then led marketing at the learning games company Lightneer, the mobile publisher Flaregames, Hatch Kids, Lockwood Publishing and the Web3 metaverse Decentraland. In 2022 I founded Transparent Eyeball, a consultancy for brand-driven games and go-to-market strategy. I led creative strategy on the Finnish National Gallery's Art Heist in Fortnite, and I am finishing a Master's in Cultural Production on cultural heritage in digital environments.

This page is a record of that work, from 2001 onwards. It is here mostly so the whole arc sits in one place.
